# Unemployment Analysis in India 📊

## Overview
This project performs an exploratory data analysis (EDA) on unemployment 
trends across Indian states from 2019 to 2020. The analysis investigates 
the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on employment, highlights regional 
disparities, and compares rural versus urban unemployment patterns.

## Objectives
- Analyze unemployment rate trends over time
- Measure the impact of COVID-19 on India's labour market
- Identify regions with the highest and lowest unemployment rates
- Compare Rural vs Urban unemployment patterns
- Examine labour participation rate trends

## Dataset
- **Source:** Kaggle
- **File:** Unemployment_in_India.csv
- **Coverage:** May 2019 – July 2020 | 28 Indian states and union territories

## Tools & Libraries
- Python 3
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## Key Findings
- Unemployment spiked sharply in **April–May 2020** following India's 
  COVID-19 lockdown
- **Bihar and Haryana** recorded the highest average unemployment rates
- Urban areas experienced more severe unemployment spikes than rural areas
  during the pandemic
- Labour participation rate dropped significantly during the lockdown period

## Visualizations
- Unemployment rate over time (line plot)
- COVID-19 impact comparison (pre vs during pandemic)
- Average unemployment by region (bar chart)
- Rural vs Urban unemployment (box plot)
- Labour participation rate over time
- Correlation heatmap
- Monthly unemployment trend
